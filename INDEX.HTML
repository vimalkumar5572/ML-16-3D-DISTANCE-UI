ℹ About

Euclidean distance is the shortest straight-line distance between two points in 3D space.

It measures how far Point A is from Point B using their X, Y and Z coordinates.

⭐ Key Properties

  • Always non-negative
  • d(A,B) = d(B,A)
  • d(A,A) = 0
  • Works in any dimension
